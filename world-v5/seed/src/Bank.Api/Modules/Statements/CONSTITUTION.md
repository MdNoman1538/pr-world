# Statements — constitution
**Purpose:** Account statements for a calendar month.
**Owner:** Customer care · **Risk:** MEDIUM
**Rules:** A statement covers the whole month (from the first day 00:00 UTC up to the first day of the next month). Opening balance + all entries = closing balance. Only the owner (or staff) may read it.
**Forbidden:** Missing or double-counting entries at month edges; statements of other customers' accounts.
